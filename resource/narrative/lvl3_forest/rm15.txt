You go left. The path becomes thinner and thornier. You swear as you barely 
escape the clutches of a particularly vicious bramble. When the path is nearly 
indistinguishable from the bushes, you begin to question the wisdom of this 
choice. 

You turn around and begin to retrace your steps, but soon realize the path is 
lost to you. You lean against the trunk of a tree before sliding to the ground 
in exhaustion. Why did you think running into the forest without supplies 
would get you anywhere? 

The crunching of branches in the distance pulls you from your misery. Through 
the brush you can see the flickering of torchlight and the sound of voices. 

Do you get closer or head in the opposite direction?
