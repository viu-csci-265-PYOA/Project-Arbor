You go right. As you continue along the road, the path widens. Strange to have 
such a well-maintained road traverse a forest in the middle of nowhere. You 
don't make it far before you feel rumbling beneath your feet. As you stand 
there listening, the rumbling grows more pronounced, almost like hoofbeats. 

Your eyes widen.

You quickly move off the path and into the brush, sliding behind a tree. 

Before long, you see a carriage appear, racing down the forest road.

Do you approach the carriage or keep yourself hidden?
