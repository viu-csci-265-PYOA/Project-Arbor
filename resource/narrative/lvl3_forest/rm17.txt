You head in the opposite direction. You ignore the noise. The chances are too 
great that you'll stumble straight into pursuing guards yet again. 

The path doesn't become any more clear and, as you continue to wander, the 
forest around you grows darker. You look up at the canopy that blots out even 
a sliver of moonlight and feel hopeless. Perhaps, it would be best to rest for 
the night and continue on in the morning.

You find a spot to rest your head and as you nod off, you think you hear 
voices again.

You jolt awake sometime later to the feeling of cold metal against your neck. 
A bandit robed in a black hat and scarf leers down at you. 

"Well, look what we have here?" The bandit drawls. "Didn't mommy or daddy ever 
tell you not to wander off the path? You never know what might be lying in 
wait."

Your journey ends.
