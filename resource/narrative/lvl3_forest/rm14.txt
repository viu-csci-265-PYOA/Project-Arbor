You're nearly out of breath by the time the tunnel dumps you in the middle of
a field. A cow moos unhappily at you as you pull yourself free and collapse 
into the grass. In the distance you can see the twinkling lights of the town. 
Turning around, the forest looms in front of you.

The Order of the Oak.

It sounds like a children's fairytale, but what choice do you have if you want 
answers? You push yourself to your feet and dust yourself off, beginning the 
trek towards the thicket. A rough hewn road winds its way deeper into the trees 
and you find yourself walking along it. After not too long, you find a fork in 
the path.

Do you go left or right?
