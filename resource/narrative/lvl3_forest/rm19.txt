You approach the carriage. Thinking this might be a chance to learn more from 
these travellers, you step out from behind the tree. You wave your arms, 
hoping the carriage will slow and you won't be trampled by the racing horses.

Luck appears to be on your side. The carriage begins to slow, rolling to a 
stop in front of you. A soldier, dressed smartly in red, jumps down from the 
carriage and marches over to you. Before you can say a word, he draws his 
sword.

"On what grounds do you dare stop a royal carriage?" the soldier growls.

"R-r-oyal carriage?" You stutter in disbelief.

The soldier's lip curls in annoyance, but at the same moment the window to the 
carriage opens and a white gloved hand waves about. 

"What's the delay, Graves?"

"Just an imbecilic peasant running into the middle of the road, your majesty."

"Well, hurry them along. We're late as it is."

"Of course, your majesty." The soldier lifts his sword once more and levels 
you with an unsettling smirk. You hold your hands up.

"Wait–"

Your journey ends.
