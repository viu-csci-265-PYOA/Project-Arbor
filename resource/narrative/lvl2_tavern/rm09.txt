You tell him that you're just travelling through. The barkeep raises an 
eyebrow as he wipes down the counter. 

"Strange time to be travelling. The roads aren't as safe as they used to be,"
he hums. 

"I can take care of myself fine," you say, gruffly. The barkeep scoffs and 
then his eyes drift down and he freezes.

"I'm sure you can," he says, mouth twisting into a frown. "That’s a fine 
amulet you have there. I haven't seen its likeness in a while."

You pull the amulet up from where it hangs against your shirt. 
"You know this symbol?" 

The barkeep nods, but his eyes are focused on something over your shoulder. 

"What are you–" You turn around and freeze as two city watchmen are headed 
your way. 

You leap to your feet and make a mad dash for what you assume is the back door 
of the tavern, but as you burst through you come face to face with half a 
dozen more guards. There’s no chance of fighting your way out this time.
