You weave your way through the shadows of a starlit city. There are few people 
on the streets at this time of night, but you can see candlelight through many 
windows.

A ruckus further down the street draws your attention as three men spill out 
into the road. They laugh, patting each other on the back as they drunkenly 
walk away. The sign above their heads reads: the Great Oak Tavern. You wander 
your way towards it, drawn by the noise and the promise of something to warm 
yourself with.

The tavern is a stark contrast to the street outside, filled to the brim with 
people and lit with warm light cast from a large fireplace set against one 
wall. In one corner a bard strums away at a lute, keeping the patrons 
entertained.

Do you approach the barkeep or attempt to listen to the gathered townsfolk?
