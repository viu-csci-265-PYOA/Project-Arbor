You flag down a server. A man holding a tray of ale bustles around 
the tavern. You lift a hand to call for his attention. When he 
finally makes his way through the crowd to your table, he sets one 
of the foamy mugs of ale in front of you. Before he turns away, you 
reach out, snagging his sleeve.

'The mood seems somber tonight,' you remark, gesturing at the table 
you’d been listening to before. The man’s eyes trail over the men 
and his mouth twists. 

'The mood has been somber for many nights, unfortunately,' the 
server says acidically.

'And why is that?' The server gives you a surprised look and you 
move quickly to explain. 'I apologize, but I’m not from around 
these parts.'
