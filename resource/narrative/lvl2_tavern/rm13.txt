You flag down a server. A man holding a tray of ale bustles around the tavern. 
You lift a hand to call for his attention. When he finally makes his way 
through the crowd to your table, he sets one of the foamy mugs of ale in front 
of you. Before he turns away, you reach out, snagging his sleeve.

“The mood seems somber tonight,” you remark, gesturing at the table you’d been 
listening to before. The man’s eyes trail over the men and his mouth twists. 

“The mood has been somber for many nights, unfortunately,” the server says 
acidically.

“And why is that?” The server gives you a surprised look and you move quickly 
to explain. “I apologize, but I’m not from around these parts.”

The server nods hesitantly. 

“Count your lucky stars then to be far enough away to escape our misfortune. 
The river isn’t the first plight to hit our farms. We’ve had storms and 
sickness across the land.”

“I suppose the crown isn’t one to send aid–” the man scoffs at your words.

“The crown is more likely to line their pockets off our misery,” he grouses. 
“Once upon a time, the order may have helped, but that was a long time ago.”

It’s the second time in as many minutes that someone has mentioned the 
mysterious order and you find your curiosity peaked.

“What is this order?” You ask. The server chuckles.

“You really are from far away aren’t you? The Order of the Oak were a group of 
knights responsible for the protection of the realm.” He waves a hand at a 
tapestry that you hadn’t noticed before. Emblazoned on it is an oak tree that 
looks suspiciously like the amulet you wear around your neck. “Unfortunately, 
they haven’t been seen in over a year.”

Seeing that the spark of interest hasn’t died, the server continues. “If you 
want to hear more about them, then I’d suggest a trip to the archives. 
Lampblack is always happy to bore visitors with a detailed account of the 
realm’s history.”
