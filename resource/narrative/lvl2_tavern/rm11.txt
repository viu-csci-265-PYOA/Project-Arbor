You attempt to listen to the townsfolk. You find yourself a spot at a table 
just out of the way enough to not catch attention, but still close enough that 
you can hear the lively conversation of the tavern-goers. While the mood had 
been particularly jovial, when you entered there's a growing rumbling at one 
of the tables nearer to you.

"Harvest? There wasn't a blasted harvest this year!" One man suddenly 
exclaims. "The river that supplies our fields ran dry long before the summer 
even began. The crops didn't stand a chance."

There are murmurs of sympathy from around the table.

"And you'd think the crown would take that into account. You'd think they'd 
know there's no money if there aren't any crops to sell for it, but no–" he 
slams a fist onto the table "– they raise the taxes instead."

The conversation breaks down as everyone chimes in.

"We need to do something–"

"They have no right!!"

'Our farm can afford to share the harvest–"

"This wouldn't have happened if the order was still around–"

The noise swells and you don't catch the rest of the sentence.

Do you move closer to hear the rest of the conversation or flag down a server?
