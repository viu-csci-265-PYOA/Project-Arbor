You approach the barkeep. You leave the tables filled with chattering patrons 
behind and pull yourself up a seat at the bar. You don’t wait long before the 
barkeep approaches you. Without a word he places a tankard of ale in front of 
you. 

“You look like you could use it,” he says.

You nod your thanks, pulling the drink towards you. Instead of moving on, the 
barkeep continues to watch you with an inquisitive eye.

“I haven’t seen you around the town before.”

You know that he expects an answer, but you mull over what story is least 
likely to get you arrested by the city’s guard again.

Do you tell him you’re just travelling through or that you have no memory of 
how you came to be there?
