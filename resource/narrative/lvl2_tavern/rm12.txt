You move closer to hear the rest of the patrons' conversation. Unfortunately, 
as you near the table your foot catches the leg of one of the wooden benches 
and sends it clattering to the ground. All at once, every eye in the room 
turns towards you. 

Maybe at first you believe that you can play it off as an accident, but one of 
the farmers at the table takes in your posture and realization flashes across 
his face. His eyes narrow.

"You were eavesdropping on us," he accuses dangerously.

You attempt to explain your way out of the situation, but it's no use. The 
patrons at the table grab hold of you and the last thing you see is someone’s 
fist before the world goes black.
