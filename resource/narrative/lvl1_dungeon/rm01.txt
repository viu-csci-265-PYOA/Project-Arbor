You search your surroundings. Your hands sift through the hay-strewn floor,
but you find nothing that might help you escape the confines of the dungeon. 
One of your hands strays near the bars and in a flash something seizes you 
by the wrist. In a panic, you yank yourself away, but the deathly white hand
holds fast and with it comes a gaunt face shrouded in a hood.
"A tree might be felled, but its roots remain." The stranger's voice is rusty
and grating like nails against stone, and the words fill you with a sense of 
dread. 

Another gnarled hand reaches through the bar and taps the place over your 
heart, surprisingly the sensation is muted by something wedged in the pocket
of your jacket. 

"Remember," the stranger says before finally releasing you and sinking back 
into the shadows. Their mutters continue, but you're too consumed by your own
thoughts to make them out.

Remember? 

You stare at your hands.

Surely, you should remember how you came to be locked in a dungeon. Or, at the 
very least, your name, but everything you try to drag up from the depths of your
psyche is blank. The stranger muttering is growing louder now and you’re jolted
as they begin yanking at the bars of their cell. In a matter of moments, two 
armoured guards appear to investigate the noise. Both guards pay you no 
attention.

Do you wait for them to leave, or attempt to incapacitate one of them?
