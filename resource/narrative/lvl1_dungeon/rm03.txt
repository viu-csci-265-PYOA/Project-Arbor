You attempt to incapacitate a guard. With the stranger’s irate shouts pulling 
their attention, it is simple to sneak up on the guard closer to your cell. 
You reach through the bars and wrap your arm around the soldier’s neck, 
wrenching them back against the bars. With your other hand, you attempt to 
reach for the keys secured to the guard’s belt, but the noise quickly alerts 
the other guard. They begin to draw their sword and you can see that this 
escape plan may be short-lived, when the stranger lunges for the other guard. 
Between the two of you, you’re able to knock the guards unconscious and free 
yourself from the cells.

You make a mad dash through the maze-like dungeon hallways, losing the 
stranger somewhere in the shadows. You’re not sure whether you should be 
grateful or worried. Finally, you break out into the moonlit road, tucking 
yourself away so that you’re hidden from pursuers. You reach into your pocket 
and pull out a thin brass disk smaller than the size of your palm. One side is 
emblazoned with a mirrored tree, branches and roots spreading out in equal 
directions. You run your thumb over the spiraling branches before flipping the 
disk over. Around the edge are symbols that you can’t decipher, but, in the 
middle, a series of familiar characters stare back at you. The name strikes a 
chord in you. Perhaps this is your first clue to who you are.
