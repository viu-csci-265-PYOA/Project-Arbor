You attempt to find a way out. make a mad dash through the maze-like dungeon 
hallways. Finally, you break out into the moonlit road, tucking yourself away 
so that you’re hidden from pursuers. You reach into your pocket and pull out a 
thin brass disk smaller than the size of your palm. One side is emblazoned 
with a mirrored tree, branches and roots spreading out in equal directions. 
You run your thumb over the spiraling branches before flipping the disk over. 
Around the edge are symbols that you can't decipher, but, in the middle, a 
series of familiar characters stare back at you. The name strikes a chord in 
you. Perhaps this is your first clue to who you are.
