You sit quietly. After a litany of tersely worded orders, the stranger retreats
back into his cell and the guards leave back down the corridor. Settling back 
against the wall, you reach into your pocket and pull out a thin brass disk 
smaller than the size of your palm. One side is emblazoned with a mirrored 
tree, branches and roots spreading out in equal directions. You run your thumb
over the spiraling branches before flipping the disk over. Around the edge are
symbols that you can’t decipher and perhaps never will. You wait in the cell, 
turning the amulet over and over, waiting for a rescue that never arrives.
