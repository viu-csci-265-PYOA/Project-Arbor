You try to open the door. You grasp the thick iron bars and give a tug, but 
the lock holds fast. You won't be getting out that way. Past the cell door an 
unlit torch is propped against the wall. The bars are just wide enough for you 
to slip your arm through. The torch is just at the edge of your reach and your 
fingertips brush against its surface. 

Grunting in frustration, you press yourself against the bars, feet scrambling 
for purchase to get yourself an inch closer. At last, your fingers make 
substantial contact and the torch teeters before tumbling to the ground. By 
some fortune, it rolls nearer to you. You drag the heavy torch back through 
the bars. The wood rod is reinforced with metal bands. 

You eye the cell door yet again. The lock might be solid, but the rusted 
hinges are not. Passing the torch through the bars near the hinge, you push 
down with all your might. The cell door groans, rusted hinges scraping at each 
other. Your effort isn't useless though, the hinges begin to separate. The 
cell door tumbles forward, falling with a resounding clang against the stone 
floor. You have won your freedom, but the sound has no doubt alerted the 
guards to your escape attempt.
