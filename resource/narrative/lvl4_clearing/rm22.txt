You choose the sword. As soon as the bandit turns his back, you dash forward. 
You leap over the trunk and roll, coming back up with a sword, secured in a 
fine leather scabbard. 

The bandit whirls around at the noise. "Who in the hell are you?" 

You unsheath the sword, rubies shimmering along its jewel-encrusted hilt, and 
brandish the blade at the bandit. The bandit chuckles, drawing his own dagger.

"So it's to be a fight, is it? Do you think you have what it takes, stranger?" 
The bandit grins and stalks forward.
