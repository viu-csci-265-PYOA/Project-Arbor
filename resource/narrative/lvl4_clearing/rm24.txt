You defend. You watch the bandit warily as you pace a circle around each other. 
The bandit smirks, flicking his dagger nimbly between his fingers, clearly 
secure in the knowledge that he has the upper hand.

The bandit's smile grows half an inch before he lunges. He's faster than you 
expect and you prepare yourself for death. To your surprise, you find yourself 
still alive enough to feel the reverberation through your arms as you bring 
the sword up to parry his attack. 

You knock the bandit away and stare at your sword hand in shock. You might 
have no memories, but some part of your body still remembers. 

The fight quickly turns in your favour.

You side-step another attack and watch him careen into the side of the 
carriage. You raise your blade and lock eyes with the significantly more 
displeased bandit.

"I guess you're a little more wily than I gave you credit for," the bandit 
sneers. You set your blade against the bandit's neck. The bandit's eyes widen 
and his mouth twitches. You firm your resolve for what needs to be done, when 
voices echo through the woods.

Your head snaps to the side as you look for the returning bandits. The 
distraction is all that is needed for a spray of dirt to catch your eyes. You 
slash out blindly and feel your sword connect. Scraping the grit from your 
eyes, you blink blearily. The first thing you see is the bandit's grin. Between 
two of his bare fingers he holds your sword.

"Perphaps not wily enough," the bandit laughs. "Your blade is dull, stranger."

Your Journey Ends.
