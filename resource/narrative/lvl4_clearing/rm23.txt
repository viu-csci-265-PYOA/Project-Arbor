You attack first. You charge the bandit, swinging the sword. The bandit lifts 
his dagger in defence, bracing the blade with a gloved hand. The weapons glance 
off each other with a loud clang.

You grit your teeth, bearing your weight down on the blade, while the bandit 
does the same. You feel his strength give just an inch and sense victory close 
at hand, when suddenly you lose your footing. You crumble to the ground and 
realize that the bandit had swept your leg out from under you. While you'd been 
paying attention to your attack, you'd forgotten to defend as well.

The bandit cackles and slashes his blade toward your heart.

Your journey ends.
