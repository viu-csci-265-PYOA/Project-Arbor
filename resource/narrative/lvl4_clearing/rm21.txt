You press yourself against the trunk of the tree as the two bandits thunder 
into the forest in search of the noise. Once they're gone, you scan the 
clearing. Apart from the clothing and jewels that litter the ground, two other 
objects catch your attention. The jewelled hilt of something that looks 
suspiciously like a sword peeks out from beneath a velvet jacket, while a few 
feet away from it lies a half opened trunk. A chain trails from the trunk 
reminiscent of a mace. You'll only have one chance at this, so you must choose 
carefully.

Do you grasp the hilt of the sword or the chain of the mace?
